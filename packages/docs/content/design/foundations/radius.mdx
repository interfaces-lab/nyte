Radius names say what a shape is, not how big it is. Five names cover containers; controls take
their radius from their own size handle.

```ts
import { radius } from "@nyte-ai/ui/styles/schema.stylex";
```

<RadiusScale />

## Rules

- Rounded containers use a `radius` name. A control reads its radius from its size handle
  (`button.radiusMd`, `input.radiusLg`), so size and corner change together.
- Nested corners stay concentric. The menu's 14px radius is its items' 8px plus its 6px
  padding.
- Under a coarse pointer, control radii grow with the control. Never branch on touch by hand.
- `radius.pill` is for shapes whose ends are always round, whatever their length.
