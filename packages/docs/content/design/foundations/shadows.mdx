## Rules

- Elevation inks are warm near-black at about 3–19% alpha in light mode. Dark mode doubles them.
- Use `shadow.shadowSm` for raised controls, `shadow.shadowLg` for popups, and `shadow.shadowXl`
  for modal popups and dialogs.
- `shadow.shadowMdOutline` adds an outside hairline in light mode, inside in dark.
- `floatingSurfaceStyles.popup` combines material, filtering, `radius.surface`, `shadow.shadowLg`,
  and `hairline.ringAfter`. `material` supplies only material and filtering.
  `modalPopup` sets only the `shadow.shadowXl` elevation variable. Compose it with a hairline.
