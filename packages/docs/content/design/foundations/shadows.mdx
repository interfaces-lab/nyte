## Rules

- The elevation shadows are inked in a warm near-black at 3 to 19 percent alpha, not neutral
  grey, so they sit on the warm gray ramp. Dark mode doubles each alpha so the shadow still reads
  on dark surfaces.
- Height follows distance from the page: controls take `shadowSm`, popups `shadowLg`, modal
  popups and dialogs `shadowXl`.
- `shadowMdOutline` adds a hairline that sits outside in light mode and inside in dark mode, where
  a drop shadow alone would vanish.
- Popups share one frame, `floatingSurfaceStyles` in `floating-surface.stylex.ts`: material,
  `radius.surface`, `shadowLg`, and an inset hairline. Compose it instead of restating them.
