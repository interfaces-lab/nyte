## Usage

- `Loader` is for loading that isn't AI work: a page, an account, a download, a process. A running
  chat, turn, subagent, or tool call shows a [Spinner](/design/spinner).
- Choose the indicator at the call site. Don't restyle one to look like the other.
- The loader fills a 16px glyph slot and takes the text color of its parent.
- Under reduced motion it holds still.
- A `Button` with `loading` shows a loader in place of its content.

## Accessibility

- The loader is `aria-hidden`. Announce what is loading with nearby text or `aria-busy` on the
  region.
