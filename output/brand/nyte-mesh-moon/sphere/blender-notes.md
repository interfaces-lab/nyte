# Mesh moon construction

Blender 5.2, Cycles. All geometry is generated by `blender-mesh-moon.py`; nothing is hand-modelled.

## Sun and crescent

The sun direction is the ASCII moon's key light, `(0.8, 0.3, -0.3)` normalised. Seen from the front camera that gives a waxing crescent with 33% of the disc lit and the terminator crossing the horizontal midline at 35% of the radius from centre.

## Lattices

Each lattice starts as a quad latitude/longitude grid over one hemisphere, generated with bmesh with the pole on +Z. The grid is 64 longitude segments by 16 latitude rings from the equator to the pole, 5.625 degrees each way. Cells at the pole are left open for the last two rings because the grid converges there and that region sits behind the limb. The object is then rotated so +Z points at the sun, which makes the equator the terminator.

A Wireframe modifier replaces the faces with struts, with boundary struts on so the terminator gets a rim. A Bevel modifier rounds the strut edges with four segments and hardened normals, so the cells stay rounded squares rather than turning into circles.

| Layer | Radius | Strut | Grid offset | Material |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Front | 0.990 | 0.028 | 0 | amber gold, metallic 0.65, roughness 0.30, coat 0.25 |
| Rear | 0.978 | 0.022 | 0.30 cells in both directions | dark bronze, metallic 0.60, roughness 0.40 |

The front gold ramps from a deeper amber at the terminator to near-white amber at the limb along the lattice's local Z, and carries a faint emission scaled by the same ramp so the crescent glows slightly on the night field. The rear layer uses a darker ramp and no emission.

## Body

A UV sphere of radius 1.0 with 256 by 128 divisions. Vertices on the lit side are pulled in by 0.032 with a smoothstep across 0.04 in the sun dot product, so the lattice sits in a recess and the front strut tops land within 0.004 of the dark limb. The step hides under the terminator strut. The material is graphite blue with metallic 0.45, roughness 0.26, coat 0.8, coat roughness 0.16, ramped brighter toward the earthshine fill direction.

## Lighting

Four area lights, all aimed at the origin with an explicit look-at matrix.

- Sun: warm rim from behind right along the sun vector, 900 W, 3.5 units square.
- Gold face: warm front-right, 220 W, light-linked to the two lattices only so it never prints a square on the glossy body.
- Earthshine: cool disk fill from the ASCII moon's fill direction, 170 W, 9 units across.
- Reflection: a 6.0 by 0.5 strip upper-left, rolled 25 degrees, 170 W. This is the diagonal reflection from the gloss study, and it reads as a soft arc on the body.

World colour is the night background `#0a0c12`. View transform is AgX with the Punchy look.

## Cameras

The front camera is orthographic at scale 3.5 for the icon, 2.3 for the mark, 0.62 for the detail. The hero camera is a 78 mm perspective orbited 20 degrees left and 4 degrees up at 8.6 units. The wordmark is a JetBrains Mono text object parented to the hero camera with 0.55 em tracking. It is hidden from glossy, diffuse and shadow rays; before that, the body reflected the four letters as four stray dots below the moon.

## Verification

Before rotation, the script casts a ray through the centre of every cell with a polar angle of at least 30 degrees. Cells nearer the pole are skipped because the grid converges there and they sit behind the limb.

| Layer | Cells | Checked | Blocked |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Front | 896 | 704 | 0 |
| Rear | 832 | 640 | 0 |

The front lattice evaluates to 181,504 faces. The four renders took between 4.8 and 6.2 seconds each at 160 samples with denoising on the Metal GPU. The icon was also inspected downscaled to 128, 64 and 32 px; the crescent and the blue glint both survive at 32 px.

Earlier passes and what changed: the first render was mirrored because the track-to quaternion rolled the camera 180 degrees, fixed with an explicit look-at. Subdivision on the wireframe turned the cells into round pores, replaced by a bevel. A stand-off shell poked past the body at the crescent tips, replaced by the recess.
