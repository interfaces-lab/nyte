# Cursor iOS study

[Open the visual guide](cursor-ios-study.html) for nine screenshot maps, nine user journeys, and recommendations for Nyte.

This study was prepared on September 12, 2026 from official screenshots, sampled demo frames, and documentation. It does not claim live app or binary inspection. The App Store version was 1.8.0; the media includes June launch layouts and the July Inbox/iPad update.

The guide labels observed controls, documented behavior, inferred icon meanings, and missing evidence separately. The remaining work is a live walkthrough of onboarding, unopened menus, failure states, permissions, and confirmation dialogs.

## Files

- `cursor-ios-study.html`: visual guide, with local images and no external runtime dependencies.
- `study-data.json`: screen descriptions, journeys, recommendations, and coverage gaps.
- `app-store-metadata.json`: app version, bundle identifier, and original screenshot URLs.
- `demo-evidence.json` and `ipad-demo-evidence.json`: requested thumbnail times and source URLs. Frames requested beyond a clip's duration may repeat its final frame; requested times are not verified playback timestamps.
- `evidence/`: published screenshots and sampled frames. The guide uses all nine screenshot panels; additional frames support research and are not an exhaustive recording.
- `*-source.html`: downloaded public pages used to locate official demo media.

No Nyte product code changed. Images belong to their original owners and are reference material, not product assets.
